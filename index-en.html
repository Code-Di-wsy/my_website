Access to the Venue

Getting to Dishui Lake

Shanghai is served by Hongqiao International Airport (SHA) and Pudong International Airport (PVG). From either airport, take Metro Line 2 to Longyang Road and transfer to Line 16 for Dishui Lake Station. A short taxi ride connects the station to the institute.

Passengers arriving at Hongqiao Terminal 1 should take Metro Line 10 to Terminal 2 and transfer to Line 2.

Pudong Airport also offers the Lingang DZ38 bus to Dishui Lake Bus Hub, followed by a short taxi ride to the institute. Check current airport bus schedules before departure.
