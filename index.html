Access to the Venue

Getting to Dishui Lake

Shanghai is served by Hongqiao International Airport (SHA) and Pudong International Airport (PVG). From either airport, take Metro Line 2 to Longyang Road and transfer to Line 16 for Dishui Lake Station. A short taxi ride connects the station to the institute.

上海虹桥机场和浦东机场均可经地铁 2 号线至龙阳路，换乘 16 号线到滴水湖站，再乘出租车前往学院。虹桥 T1 旅客需先乘 10 号线到 T2 换乘。

Pudong Airport also offers the Lingang DZ38 bus to Dishui Lake Bus Hub. Fixed departures: T2 at 08:00 / 18:30; T1 at 08:05 / 18:35. Check current airport bus schedules before departure.
浦东机场亦可乘临港 DZ38 线至滴水湖枢纽站。固定班次较少，其他班次请查询“临港出行”预约信息。
